What Is a Virtual Assistant?

A virtual assistant, or VA, is a remote professional who handles administrative, technical or creative tasks for a business — email and calendar management, customer support, data entry, social media and more — without needing a desk in your office. For busy founders and small teams, a VA offers the support of an employee with far more flexibility and lower overhead than a traditional hire. At IT Freelancers Clients, we match businesses with verified virtual assistants suited to their specific tasks and industry, from general administrative support to specialized e-commerce or real estate VA work.

Who Needs a Virtual Assistant

Our virtual assistant services are built for busy founders drowning in email and scheduling, small businesses that need reliable customer support without a full-time hire, and e-commerce sellers who need ongoing data entry, research or store support. If administrative work is eating into time you should be spending on growth, a virtual assistant usually pays for itself quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours does a virtual assistant work?

Arrangements range from a few hours a week to full-time support, depending on your workload and budget — this is scoped during the initial needs assessment.

Can a virtual assistant handle industry-specific tasks?

Yes, we match VAs with relevant experience for real estate, e-commerce, podcasting and other specialized needs covered in our guide series.

What tools do your virtual assistants use?

VAs work within your existing tools and workflows — email, calendar, CRM or project management software — rather than requiring you to adopt new systems.

Is there a minimum commitment period?

We offer flexible arrangements starting on a trial basis, so you can confirm fit before committing to a longer-term engagement.

How do I communicate tasks to my virtual assistant?

Most clients use a mix of email, task management tools, or direct messaging — we help set up whichever workflow fits how you already work.

What if I need more hours than originally agreed?

Hours can be adjusted as your needs change — we check in regularly so scope and workload stay aligned with what you're actually asking for.
