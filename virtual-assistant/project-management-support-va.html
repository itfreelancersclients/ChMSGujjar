Project Management Support From a Virtual Assistant

By IT Freelancers Clients | Updated July 2026 | 7 min read

Keeping projects on track requires consistent follow-up and tracking that can easily slip during busy periods. A Virtual Assistant with project coordination skills helps keep deadlines, tasks, and communication organized.

Quick Summary: A project-support VA tracks deadlines, coordinates task updates, and maintains project documentation, helping keep initiatives on schedule without requiring a dedicated project manager.

1. Tracking Deadlines and Deliverables

Projects with multiple moving parts benefit from consistent tracking — a VA can maintain an updated view of what's due, what's complete, and what's at risk of slipping.

2. Common Project Support Tasks

Popular Project Management VA Services

  • Task and deadline tracking
  • Project management tool updates
  • Status report preparation
  • Meeting notes and action item follow-up
  • Stakeholder communication coordination
  • Document and file organization

3. Following Up on Action Items

Action items from meetings often get forgotten without a dedicated follow-up process — a VA can track and gently follow up on these commitments.

4. Supporting Without Replacing Strategic Decisions

A project support VA typically handles tracking and coordination, while strategic project decisions remain with the project owner or manager.

Pro Tip: Give your VA edit access to your project management tool from day one — this lets them update statuses directly rather than relaying information through you first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Virtual Assistant replace a project manager?

For smaller projects, a VA can handle much of the coordination, though larger or more complex projects may still benefit from a dedicated project manager.

What project management tools can a VA typically use?

Most VAs are comfortable with common tools like Trello, Asana, or Monday.com, often learning a specific tool quickly if needed.
