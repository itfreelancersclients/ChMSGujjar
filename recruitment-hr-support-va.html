Recruitment & HR Support From a Virtual Assistant

By IT Freelancers Clients | Updated July 2026 | 6 min read

Hiring and basic HR administration take significant time away from core business activities. A Virtual Assistant experienced in recruitment support can manage much of this process, from job postings to interview scheduling.

Quick Summary: A recruitment-focused VA manages job postings, initial candidate screening, and interview scheduling, streamlining the hiring process for growing businesses.

1. Managing Job Postings and Applications

Posting openings across multiple platforms and organizing incoming applications is time-consuming — a VA can manage this consistently, keeping the pipeline organized.

2. Common Recruitment & HR VA Tasks

Popular Recruitment Support Services

  • Job posting across platforms
  • Resume screening and organization
  • Interview scheduling coordination
  • Candidate communication
  • Onboarding document preparation
  • Basic HR record keeping

3. Keeping Candidates Informed Throughout

Prompt, professional communication with candidates — even those not moving forward — protects the employer's reputation and candidate experience.

4. Supporting Onboarding Once Hired

Once a candidate is hired, a VA can help prepare onboarding paperwork and materials, easing the transition for both the new hire and the hiring manager.

Pro Tip: Provide your VA with clear screening criteria upfront — this allows them to pre-filter candidates effectively before you review the shortlist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Virtual Assistant make hiring decisions?

No — a VA typically supports the process through screening and coordination, while final hiring decisions remain with the business owner or hiring manager.

Can a VA handle confidential HR information?

With proper confidentiality agreements in place, many VAs are trusted with sensitive HR administrative tasks.
