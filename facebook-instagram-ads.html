Facebook & Instagram Ads: Getting the Targeting Right

By IT Freelancers Clients  |  Updated July 2026  |  7 min read

Meta's ad platform offers powerful targeting, but misused settings quietly waste budget. This guide covers how to structure campaigns that reach the right audience efficiently.

Quick summary: Let the algorithm optimize with broader targeting rather than over-narrowing audiences, and always structure campaigns around a clear conversion objective from the start.

1. Avoid Over-Narrowing Your Audience

Stacking too many interest and demographic filters shrinks the audience pool so much that the algorithm struggles to find enough people to optimize delivery effectively.

2. Choose the Right Campaign Objective From the Start

Why objective selection shapes delivery

Selecting "traffic" as an objective when the real goal is purchases trains the algorithm to optimize for the wrong outcome, even if the ad creative itself is strong.

3. Use Lookalike Audiences From Real Customer Data

Building lookalike audiences from actual purchasing customers, rather than just page followers, typically produces significantly better-qualified traffic.

4. Give the Algorithm Time Before Judging Performance

New ad sets need a learning period to optimize delivery. Making frequent changes during this phase resets learning and can hurt long-term performance.

Pro tip: Upload your best customer list as a custom audience and build a lookalike from it before testing broader interest-based targeting — it's often the fastest path to efficient results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should Meta ad targeting be narrow or broad?

Generally broader, letting the algorithm's optimization do the narrowing rather than over-restricting manually upfront.

How long is the ad learning phase?

It varies by ad set, but generally requires a set number of conversion events before delivery stabilizes.
