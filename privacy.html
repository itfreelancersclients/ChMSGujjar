Privacy Policy

How IT Freelancers Clients collects, uses, and protects your information.

Our Commitment

IT Freelancers Clients respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us.

How We Use Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Any information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiries and deliver our services.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and see which content is most useful to visitors. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymised usage data (such as pages visited, time on site, and general location). This data helps us improve the site — it does not identify you personally. When you first visit, a cookie banner lets you accept or decline analytics cookies; declining means no analytics tracking runs during your visit.

Contact & Social Media

If you contact us through WhatsApp or social media, your information will only be used to respond to your inquiry and will not be shared elsewhere.

Cookie Consent

When you first visit this site, you'll see a short banner asking whether you're okay with analytics cookies. If you decline, Google Analytics does not run on your visit. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

Your Agreement

By using this website, you agree to this Privacy Policy. We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices.
