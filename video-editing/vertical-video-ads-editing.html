Vertical Video Ads: Editing for Paid Social Campaigns

By IT Freelancers Clients  |  Updated July 2026  |  6 min read

Paid vertical ads on Meta and TikTok are judged on cost-per-result, not just views. This guide covers the editing decisions that improve ad performance and lower acquisition costs.

Quick summary: Front-load the hook within 2 seconds, design for sound-off viewing with captions and text, and always create multiple hook variations for testing.

1. The Hook Must Land in the First 2 Seconds

Paid social platforms charge for impressions regardless of whether someone watches further, making the opening frame the single most important editing decision in an ad.

2. Design Every Ad Assuming Sound Is Off

Why silent-first design protects ad spend

A large share of paid social impressions happen with sound muted by default. Ads relying entirely on voiceover to communicate the offer waste significant ad spend on viewers who never hear it.

3. Create Multiple Hook Variations for Testing

Editing 3–5 different opening hooks from the same core footage allows for A/B testing which hook drives the lowest cost-per-result before scaling ad spend.

4. Keep the Call-to-Action Visually Repeated

Rather than showing the offer or call-to-action once, repeating it as a text overlay throughout the ad ensures viewers who drop off partway still see the key message.

Pro tip: Treat every ad edit as a hypothesis to test, not a final answer — the best-performing hook is rarely the one that felt strongest during editing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a paid vertical ad be?

6–15 seconds typically performs best for cost-efficient paid social campaigns.

Do captions matter for paid ads specifically?

Yes — with most impressions happening sound-off, captions are essential for the message to land at all.
