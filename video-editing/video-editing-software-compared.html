Video Editing Software Compared: Premiere, DaVinci & Final Cut

By IT Freelancers Clients  |  Updated July 2026  |  7 min read

Choosing the right editing software affects workflow speed, collaboration, and cost. This guide compares the leading platforms so creators and businesses can pick the right fit.

Quick summary: Premiere Pro leads in industry-standard collaboration, DaVinci Resolve excels at color grading with a strong free tier, and Final Cut Pro offers speed advantages for Mac-based workflows.

1. Premiere Pro: The Industry Collaboration Standard

Premiere Pro remains the most widely used option in professional and agency settings, making it the easiest choice when collaborating with other editors or clients using the same software.

2. DaVinci Resolve: Best Color Grading, Strong Free Tier

Why colorists often prefer Resolve

DaVinci Resolve's color grading tools are considered industry-leading, and its generous free version makes it an accessible entry point for editors building color grading skills.

3. Final Cut Pro: Speed on Mac Hardware

Final Cut Pro's optimization for Apple hardware often results in faster render and export times on Mac systems, appealing to editors working within an all-Apple workflow.

4. Choose Based on Workflow, Not Just Features

The "best" software depends heavily on collaboration needs, existing hardware, and whether color grading or editing speed matters more for a given project type.

Pro tip: If working with clients or teams regularly, prioritize whichever software they already use — compatibility saves far more time than any single feature advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DaVinci Resolve really free?

Yes, with a robust free version, though a paid Studio version unlocks additional advanced features.

Which software is best for beginners?

DaVinci Resolve's free tier and structured interface make it a strong, cost-free starting point for new editors.
