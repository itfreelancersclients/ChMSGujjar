AI Tools in Video Editing: How They're Changing the Workflow

By IT Freelancers Clients  |  Updated July 2026  |  7 min read

AI-powered tools now handle transcription, rough cuts, and even basic color matching in seconds. This guide covers where these tools genuinely speed up editing, and where a skilled human editor still makes the real difference.

Quick summary: AI tools excel at repetitive, time-consuming tasks like transcription and silence removal, but creative pacing, storytelling, and emotional judgment still depend on a skilled human editor.

1. Let AI Handle Transcription and Captions

Automated transcription tools now produce highly accurate captions in a fraction of the time manual transcription would take, freeing up editor time for more creative work.

2. Use AI for First-Pass Rough Cuts

Why AI rough cuts still need a human pass

AI tools can automatically remove silences and filler words to produce a workable first draft, but the resulting cut typically still needs a human editor's pass for pacing and storytelling judgment.

3. Rely on AI for Repetitive Technical Tasks

Tasks like audio leveling, basic noise removal, and initial color matching across multiple clips can often be automated reliably, saving significant manual technical work.

4. Keep Creative Decisions With a Human Editor

Decisions about emotional pacing, which moments deserve emphasis, and overall narrative structure still depend on human judgment that current AI tools can't reliably replicate.

Pro tip: Use AI tools to handle the tedious first 80% of a rough cut, then spend your own editing time on the final 20% that actually determines whether the video feels genuinely engaging.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will AI replace video editors?

Unlikely in full — AI handles repetitive technical tasks well, but creative storytelling and pacing judgment remain a human strength.

What video editing tasks are safest to automate with AI?

Transcription, silence removal, and basic audio leveling are currently the most reliable tasks to hand off to AI tools.
