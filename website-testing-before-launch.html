Testing Your Website Before Launch

By IT Freelancers Clients  |  Updated July 2026  |  6 min read

Launching a website without thorough testing risks visible bugs and broken functionality reaching real visitors. This guide covers what to test before going live.

Quick summary: Test across multiple browsers and devices, verify all forms and interactive elements function correctly, and check for broken links and missing content before the actual launch.

1. Test Across Multiple Browsers and Devices

A site that works perfectly in one browser can behave differently in another due to rendering differences — testing across the major browsers and common devices catches these discrepancies before launch.

2. Verify Every Form and Interactive Element

Why untested forms are a common launch-day surprise

A contact form or checkout process that appears fine visually can still fail to actually submit or process correctly — functionally testing every interactive element prevents this common oversight.

3. Check for Broken Links and Missing Content

Placeholder text or broken internal links accidentally left in from development can slip through if not deliberately checked before launch, creating an unprofessional first impression.

4. Test Under Realistic Traffic and Load Conditions

A site that performs well during light development testing might behave differently under the traffic load of an actual public launch — load testing beforehand can reveal capacity issues early.

Pro tip: Create a simple pre-launch checklist covering browsers, devices, forms, and links, and go through it methodically rather than relying on memory before every launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it enough to test a website in just one browser?

No — rendering differences between browsers mean thorough testing should cover all major browsers your audience is likely to use.

Should load testing be done before every website launch?

It's particularly important for sites expecting significant traffic at launch, though even smaller sites benefit from basic performance checks.
