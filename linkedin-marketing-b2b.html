LinkedIn Marketing for B2B: Reaching Decision Makers

By IT Freelancers Clients  |  Updated July 2026  |  6 min read

LinkedIn remains the dominant platform for reaching business decision-makers. This guide covers how to build a presence and run campaigns that generate qualified B2B leads.

Quick summary: Personal profile content often outperforms company page posts for reach, and thoughtful comments on relevant industry posts build visibility as effectively as original content.

1. Leverage Personal Profiles Over Company Pages

LinkedIn's algorithm consistently favors content from personal profiles over company pages, making employee advocacy a powerful, often underused reach strategy.

2. Comment Strategically on Industry Conversations

Why thoughtful comments build as much visibility as posts

A genuinely insightful comment on a well-performing industry post can reach more relevant decision-makers than a mediocre original post buried in the feed.

3. Use LinkedIn's Native Document and Carousel Formats

Native carousel and document posts tend to receive higher engagement than external links, which the algorithm treats as sending traffic away from the platform.

4. Combine Organic Presence With Targeted Ads

A consistent organic presence builds trust that makes LinkedIn ads to the same audience convert noticeably better than cold outreach to strangers.

Pro tip: Spend 15 minutes daily genuinely engaging with your target audience's posts before publishing your own content — this warms up the algorithm's perception of your account activity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should B2B companies focus on company pages or personal profiles?

Personal profiles, especially from leadership and sales teams, typically achieve significantly higher organic reach.

Are LinkedIn ads worth it for B2B?

Yes, particularly when combined with an existing organic presence that builds familiarity before the ad is seen.
